**The situation.** Six neighbours in one building have run a جمعية for two
years — everyone pays ١٥٬٠٠٠ a month, one person takes the whole ٩٠٬٠٠٠. No
contract. No bank. No paper except a school exercise book kept by the woman on
the ground floor. Tonight the pot is Hoda's. Her daughter's حفلة is in ten days.

At 8:10 the accountant on the third floor says the money should be counted in
front of everyone before it is handed over.

At 9:00 the envelope is not in the drawer.

**The shape of the case.** It opens as a theft with an obvious prize — ٩٠٬٠٠٠ in
cash in a room with four people who all need money. Halfway through, the money
is found, unopened, inside the flat, and the case reclassifies:

> **Act I** — مين كان عايز الـ٩٠٬٠٠٠؟
> **Act II** — محدش خدها. يبقى مين كان خايف من العدّ؟
> **Act III** — ومين كان خايف إن الـ٤٥٬٠٠٠ تتفهم؟

The third question is the case. Everything after E01 exists to move the table
from the first question to the third.

### 2.2 What actually happened

**Mostafa moved the envelope at ~8:15.** He took it from the buffet drawer and
pushed it behind the water heater on the kitchen balcony. He did not leave the
flat with it. He did not open it. He never intended to keep it.

**He did it to stop the count.** He has not paid for three months and did not
pay tonight. A public count produces ٧٥٬٠٠٠ against an expected ٩٠٬٠٠٠, and the
only question in the room afterwards is *who didn't pay*.

**Souad has been covering him.** For three months she put her own ١٥٬٠٠٠ into
the pot so that each recipient received a full ٩٠٬٠٠٠, and wrote **تم** beside
his name each time. She could not raise it every Thursday on time, so on those
three months the recipient was paid **in two parts, about a week apart** — and
she noted the second date beside the **تم**. Those three double-dates are the
only irregularity in twenty-four months of grid, and they are on the page.

**She could not do it a fourth time.** Ten days ago she told Mostafa so. He did
not believe her. Tonight she put ٧٥٬٠٠٠ in the envelope with a folded note under
the elastic band, addressed to Hoda, promising the remaining ١٥٬٠٠٠ next
Thursday. Her plan was to hand it over privately at the door. **The public count
destroyed her plan and his in the same sentence.**

**Nabil's arithmetic is correct; his interpretation is inverted.** He is an
accountant. Over the last three months two recipients told him they had been
paid in two instalments, a week apart, and Souad's excuse each time was travel.
He recognised the pattern as a cash-flow gap, sized it at three months of one
share — **٤٥٬٠٠٠** — and concluded that Souad has been using the pot as a float
for herself. Every observation is true. The direction of the money is the one
thing he got wrong, and it is the only thing that matters.

**Nabil has not had a job since June.** He leaves the building at 7:40 every
morning with his bag and returns at 5. Only his wife knows. He wants his money
out of the جمعية before anyone finds out, and he cannot say so.

**Hoda paid ٤٠٬٠٠٠ on the hall eleven days ago** with money borrowed at 10%
against a pot she had not yet received. Balance ٥٥٬٠٠٠, due in ten days. At 7:40
she was alone in the salon, opened the drawer, saw the envelope, closed it, and
has decided not to mention it because it makes her the suspect. **She did not
know the envelope was short.** She also, at 8:13, sided with Nabil and told
Souad to let them count it.

**Two years ago Hoda missed a month.** Her brother told her he had taken care of
it. He never did. Souad paid the ١٢٬٠٠٠ and let the credit stand where it had
landed, because in that stairwell a woman whose brother looks after her is a
different woman from one who couldn't pay.

### 2.3 What the case means

The جمعية never ran on money. It ran on one woman absorbing everybody's bad
month so that nobody in that building ever had to be *seen* having one. Nothing
was stolen tonight. The thing that broke it was a reasonable man asking to see
the numbers.

**Alibi geometry.** Every one of the four has an unexplained gap between 7:25
and 8:25 and an innocent reason for it that is humiliating to give. That
asymmetry — *cheap facts are useless, useful facts are expensive* — is the
discussion economy of the entire case.

**Two clocks.** The envelope's window is 8:05→9:00 (Souad's glasses). The
*motive* window is 8:10→9:00 (the count). They differ by five minutes and that
difference is the solution.

## 5. Four-player cast

**Selected: سعاد · هدى · مصطفى · نبيل.**

Chosen on information distribution, not convenience. The four decisive facts in
the case are held one each, by four different people, with zero overlap:

| Decisive fact | Sole holder |
|---|---|
| Where the envelope is / who moved it | **مصطفى** |
| Someone has not paid for three months, and I covered it | **سعاد** |
| The envelope was in the drawer at 7:40 | **هدى** |
| Mostafa came out of the flat at 8:20 | **نبيل** |

Removing any one of the four removes a unique load-bearing fact. Removing either
of the other two removes nothing the four-player version needs.

### 5.1 What the absent two take with them

**أم نادر (absent — at her daughter's in Tanta; her share came Tuesday).** She
carries the memory of Hoda's missed month two years ago. In the 4-player version
that layer becomes **reveal-only**: it is *evidenced* (the ١٢٬٠٠٠ row on E01 f3,
dated to the ١٢٬٠٠٠ era by E02) but not *attributable* at the table. Players can
and should work out that Souad covered someone else once before; they cannot
work out that it was Hoda. **That is intentional — it is the one thing a correct
vote does not buy you.**

**رمضان (absent — paid on Tuesday).** He carries a second, corroborating
stairwell sighting. Nabil's 8:20 sighting is already designed as *optional
confirmation, not a load-bearing beam* (§15.2), so removing the corroboration of
an optional step removes nothing.

**Verification:** the nine-step reconstruction in §16.4 closes with four players
and does not reference either absent character at any step.

### 5.2 Their in-fiction absence

Both absences are stated in the case intro, not invented at the table, and both
are ordinary: one member is travelling, one paid early. A جمعية where two of six
hand their share over on a different day is a normal جمعية.

### 6.1 سعاد عبد الحميد — Souad Abdel-Hamid, 56

**PUBLIC IDENTITY** — Ground floor. Retired school administrator, widowed nine
years. Has run the جمعية for two years. In this building, *"سعاد ماشية الجمعية"*
is a statement about her character, not her admin duties.

**PERSONALITY** — Unhurried. Deflects with hospitality: a question she doesn't
want is answered with tea. Reaches for proverbs when cornered. The kindness is
completely real, and it is also how she holds her position.

**RELATIONSHIPS** — Knew Hoda's mother; treats Hoda as a younger sister who must
never be pitied. Calls Mostafa "يا ابني" and means it — his mother was her
closest friend. Respects Nabil, finds him exhausting, and is about to be accused
by him.

**WHAT THEY KNOW** — ① The envelope was in the drawer at 8:05. Mostafa has not
paid for four months including tonight. She has put ٤٥٬٠٠٠ of her own into the
pot. She paid three recipients in two parts because she couldn't raise it in
time. Tonight's envelope holds ٧٥٬٠٠٠ and a note for Hoda. Two years ago she
paid Hoda's ١٢٬٠٠٠.

**WHAT THEY BELIEVE** — That naming Mostafa in that room finishes him in the
building, and that a man finished on his own staircase does not recover.

**WHAT THEY SUSPECT** — She is near-certain within twenty minutes that it was
Mostafa. She says nothing, and her silence is visible.

**WHAT THEY ARE MISUNDERSTANDING** — ② That her look at 7:14 told Mostafa *don't
worry, I'll handle it*. It told him she still had it under control. She has no
idea he arrived that evening intending to confess to her, or that her
reassurance is what let him relax straight into the ambush at 8:10.

**WHAT THEY ARE HIDING** — ③ All of the above, and one thing more: the ٤٥٬٠٠٠
came from selling her gold. The building watched her come out of الصاغة three
months ago and drew its own conclusions.

**WHAT THEY WANT** — ④ For the night to end with Hoda paid and nobody publicly
shamed — in that order — and she now knows the two are incompatible.

**WHAT THEY FEAR** — Being the woman who wrote **تم** next to a lie. Her entire
standing rests on her word not needing paper. The notebook is the paper, and it
is against her.

**WHAT THEY CAN TRUTHFULLY REVEAL** — The 8:05 sighting (free). That she wrote
the last-page column herself (costly). That the envelope was short before it
went in the drawer (very costly). Who it was for (she will not, without being
cornered by E04).

**WHAT THEY DO NOT KNOW** — That Mostafa hid it rather than took it. That Nabil
has been keeping his own figures. That Nabil is unemployed. That Hoda borrowed
at interest. That Hoda saw the envelope at 7:40.

### 6.2 هدى — Hoda, 41

**PUBLIC IDENTITY** — Second floor. Runs a small tailoring business from the
front room. Widowed. Her daughter's حفلة is in ten days and the whole building
has been asked about the dress.

**PERSONALITY** — Fast. Answers a question with a question. Defensive before
she has been accused, which reads as guilt and isn't. Funny when she isn't
frightened, and she has not been unfrightened in eleven days.

**RELATIONSHIPS** — Close to Souad in the specific way of someone who has needed
her. Openly irritated by Nabil over the turn order. Fond of Mostafa; treats him
as a kid, which is exactly why he could not bear to be exposed in front of her.

**WHAT THEY KNOW** — ① She was alone in the salon 7:40–7:46 and the envelope was
in the drawer. She paid ٤٠٬٠٠٠ on the hall eleven days ago with borrowed money
at 10%. The balance is ٥٥٬٠٠٠ and due in ten days. At 8:13 she told Souad to let
them count it.

**WHAT THEY BELIEVE** — That Nabil engineered tonight to embarrass her, because
he asked for her month two months ago and Souad refused him.

**WHAT THEY SUSPECT** — Nabil. Loudly, early, and with an actual reason — which
is what makes her wrong rather than stupid.

**WHAT THEY ARE MISUNDERSTANDING** — ② That her brother paid her missed month
two years ago. He said he did. He didn't. She has never checked, because
checking would mean saying the words *I missed a month* out loud to Souad.

**WHAT THEY ARE HIDING** — ③ The borrowing. The 7:40 visit to the drawer. And,
underneath both, that she is still roughly ٩٬٠٠٠ short even if she receives the
full pot, and has been quietly asking what her machine would fetch.

**WHAT THEY WANT** — ④ The ٩٠٬٠٠٠ tonight, without one person on that staircase
learning how close to the edge she is.

**WHAT THEY FEAR** — Being pitied. Precisely: becoming the woman the building
takes up a collection for.

**WHAT THEY CAN TRUTHFULLY REVEAL** — That the envelope was in the drawer at
7:40 — which halves the window and simultaneously puts her alone in the room
with it.

**WHAT THEY DO NOT KNOW** — That the envelope was ١٥٬٠٠٠ short. That there is a
note inside it addressed to her. That Souad paid her month two years ago. That
Souad has been covering Mostafa. That Nabil is unemployed.

### 6.3 مصطفى — Mostafa, 33

**PUBLIC IDENTITY** — Fourth floor. Works in a mobile phone shop and has spent a
year telling people he is about to open a second one. Son of Souad's closest
friend, who died four years ago. Half-raised in the ground-floor flat.

**PERSONALITY** — Loud, warm, first to carry something heavy for somebody. Jokes
harder the more cornered he is, which is a tell anyone at the table can learn
inside ten minutes. *"والله العظيم"* arrives about four sentences before a lie.

**RELATIONSHIPS** — Calls Souad "يا طنط" and means it as family. Genuinely likes
Hoda and genuinely wants her to get her money. Finds Nabil humiliating to stand
next to.

**WHAT THEY KNOW** — ① He moved the envelope and where it is. He has not paid
for four months. A phone-import deal collapsed in the spring and took
everything. Souad told him ten days ago that she could not cover him again.

**WHAT THEY BELIEVE** — That Souad covered him *completely and easily*, and that
the hole is only ١٥٬٠٠٠ — tonight's.

**WHAT THEY ARE MISUNDERSTANDING** — ② The size and the cost. He does not know
it is ٤٥٬٠٠٠. He does not know she sold her gold. He does not know she ever did
it for anyone else. He read her look at 7:14 as *don't worry* and stopped
worrying, which is why 8:10 hit him with no defence prepared.

**WHAT THEY ARE HIDING** — ③ All of it, and above all that there is no second
shop, there was never going to be, and the money for it is gone.

**WHAT THEY WANT** — ④ To reach the end of the night still being the man who
carries things upstairs for people. He would rather three neighbours suspected
him of theft than six neighbours knew he was broke.

**WHAT THEY FEAR** — Not being called a thief. Being *the reason سعاد is exposed
as a liar in her own handwriting.* His shame is braided into hers, and that is
the specific thing he cannot survive.

**WHAT THEY CAN TRUTHFULLY REVEAL** — Where the envelope is. That is his entire
game, and every minute he doesn't makes saying it more expensive.

**WHAT THEY DO NOT KNOW** — That Souad already knows it was him. That Nabil saw
him leave the flat at 8:20. That she only ever covered part of it and is now out
of savings. That the envelope he hid was already short — **he risked everything
to conceal a number he had never actually seen.**

### 6.4 نبيل — Nabil, 49

**PUBLIC IDENTITY** — Third floor. Accountant. Owns the folder with the
building's water bills going back six years. Punctual to the minute.

**PERSONALITY** — Precise. Slides into semi-formal Arabic under stress —
*"المفروض يكون في ورق"* — which the others read as pomposity. Not cold; rigid,
and rigid because he is frightened. He has been humourless for exactly two
months and nobody has connected the dates.

**RELATIONSHIPS** — Formal with Souad, whom he respects and is about to accuse.
Friction with Hoda over the turn order. Contempt for Mostafa that he believes he
conceals.

**WHAT THEY KNOW** — ① He demanded the count at 8:10. He was on the stairwell
8:15–8:25 and saw Mostafa come out of the flat at 8:20. Two recipients told him
they were paid in two parts a week apart, in each of the last three months, and
that Souad's stated reason was travel. He sized the gap at three months of one
share — ٤٥٬٠٠٠. He has had no salary since June.

**WHAT THEY BELIEVE** — That Souad has been running the pot as her own float —
taking, using, replacing. Not malice; he thinks he is the only person willing to
look at it.

**WHAT THEY SUSPECT** — Souad, primarily and confidently. Mostafa, vaguely,
because he saw him — but a phone-shop kid is not who you accuse of a ٩٠٬٠٠٠
envelope, and he knows exactly how that would sound coming from him.

**WHAT THEY ARE MISUNDERSTANDING** — ② The direction. Every observation he made
is true. Money was late; the lateness was regular; the size is exactly one
share. He assumed a hole being temporarily filled means someone is emptying it.
It means someone is filling it from outside.

**WHAT THEY ARE HIDING** — ③ The unemployment. That he asked Souad two months
ago to move his turn forward and was refused. And that the reason he was
standing in the stairwell at 8:15 pretending to have no signal was a recruiter —
the one fact that would clear the timeline.

**WHAT THEY WANT** — ④ To be shown to be right, to get his contributions out,
and to be gone from this building's finances before anyone finds out he has no
salary.

**WHAT THEY FEAR** — The word *عاطل* attached to his name on his own staircase.

**WHAT THEY CAN TRUTHFULLY REVEAL** — The 8:20 sighting. The most valuable
single observation in the room, producible only by explaining why he was
standing on the stairs inventing a signal problem.

**WHAT THEY DO NOT KNOW** — That the ٤٥٬٠٠٠ went *in*. That Mostafa hasn't paid.
That Hoda borrowed. That Souad sold her gold. That the envelope was short before
it entered the drawer.

### E01 — دفتر الجمعية · `notebook` · `requires: []`

**TITLE** — دفتر الجمعية *(The Notebook)*

**PURPOSE** — Establish that this entire system is documented by one person's
handwriting, and put the number **٤٥٬٠٠٠** on the table in the first three
minutes without anyone asserting it.

**WHAT PLAYERS INITIALLY SEE** — A school exercise book, plastic cover, corners
gone soft. Two years of Thursdays.

**WHAT INTERACTION REVEALS** *(3 fragments — surface sketch, not final copy)*

1. **من جوه الغلاف** — six names, the months in a grid, one hand, one pen.

## 10. Numerical fairness — how the table derives ٤٥٬٠٠٠ without Nabil

**Requirement:** players must reconstruct the discrepancy independently.
Nabil's arithmetic must be *corroboration*, never the source.

**Derivation, from objects only:**

| Step | Source | Operation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | E01 f3 | Read four amounts: `١٢٬٠٠٠ · ١٥٬٠٠٠ · ١٥٬٠٠٠ · ١٥٬٠٠٠` |
| 2 | E01 f3 | The three ١٥٬٠٠٠ rows are dated to the last three months → **١٥٬٠٠٠ × ٣ = ٤٥٬٠٠٠** |
| 3 | E02 f1 | Share was ١٢٬٠٠٠ until twelve months ago → the ١٢٬٠٠٠ row is **from the old era**, ~two years back |
| 4 | E01 f2 | Those same three months are the only three in two years paid in **two parts** |
| 5 | E05 f2 | Tonight's envelope is short by **١٥٬٠٠٠ — exactly one share** |
| 6 | 2 + 5 | The recent anomaly is *one member's share, four months running* |
| 7 | E04 f2 | An outside party states Mostafa has been three months behind |
| 8 | E05 f3 | The organiser is promising to make up ١٥٬٠٠٠ **from herself** |

Steps 1–2 put ٤٥٬٠٠٠ on the table by addition. Step 5 gives it a unit — *one

**Nabil is required at none of these steps.** His contribution is that he got

**The number is genuinely ambiguous until step 8.** ٤٥٬٠٠٠ leaving and ٤٥٬٠٠٠

ambiguity is the case, and it is honest: no evidence lies about it, the page
simply has no header.

figure in the case is a whole multiple of a share, which is what lets a table do
this arithmetic out loud without a calculator.

## 11. Mostafa's 8:15 — motivation specification

The one link in the chain that is psychological rather than logical, and
therefore the only one a player can refuse to accept. Specified in eight beats
so the eventual writing has no room to fudge it.

**1 — He arrived intending to confess.** 6:52, in the stairwell, he wrote the
message to Souad. It exists, it is timestamped, it is on E04. **A man who drafts
a confession ninety minutes before a theft is not a thief**, and the object
proves it.

**2 — He didn't send it, because she looked at him.** 7:14. Her look meant *I
know, don't panic*. He read it as *I've handled it, as usual*. He put the phone
away and relaxed. **Souad's kindness is what removed his defences.**

**3 — 8:10 destroys two plans in one sentence.** His (confess privately after
everyone leaves) and hers (hand it over at the door with a note). Neither of
them can say a word about why the count is a problem without saying the thing
the count would reveal.

**4 — He watches her fail.** She refuses at 8:11. She refuses again at 8:13.
Then Hoda — the person he most wants not to be exposed in front of — sides with
Nabil, and the room agrees. **He is the only person present who knows what the
count will produce, and he has just watched the one person who could stop it be
overruled.**

**5 — The shame is not his own.** He is not primarily afraid of being called a
man who didn't pay. He is afraid of being **the reason سعاد is exposed as
someone who wrote تم next to a lie, in her own handwriting, in front of the
building.** That fear is larger than his own humiliation and it is the fear that
actually moves his hands.

**6 — The window is ninety seconds and the door is open.** Tea is being made.
Two people are on the balcony. He says he's getting his charger. It is a flat he
has been in and out of since he was nine. He does not plan; he moves.

**7 — The plan is not "keep it." The plan is "not tonight."** He expects the
evening to end unresolved, and expects to produce it — or let Souad find it —
in the morning. He genuinely believes he has until dawn to fix this. **He has
about twenty minutes.**

**8 — Why he cannot undo it.** By 9:20 the room has closed. *"أنا لقيتها"* is
now a confession. Every minute makes the truth more expensive than the lie,
which is the oldest and most human trap there is. **He is not being clever. He
is being late.**

**The proof of his innocence is geographic.** He held ٩٠٬٠٠٠ in cash for forty
seconds with an open door six metres away and a working set of stairs. He put it
behind a water heater in the same flat, unopened. He never even learned it was
short. **He risked his standing in that building to conceal a number he had
never seen.**

**Writing constraint for WS-3:** the reveal must never call him a thief, must
never call him stupid, and must not soften what it cost Souad. He did a small
frightened thing and could not take it back. That is all, and it has to be
enough.

## 14. Final decision

**Engine constraint.** `DECISION_QUESTION` is a global constant —
**"Who do you trust least?"** ([`src/engine/voting.ts:30`](src/engine/voting.ts:30)) —
not a per-case field. Votes target a character; `interpretVote` compares the
outcome to `truth.immediateAnswerCharacterId`.

**Case 002 Arabic rendering (sketch):** «مين أقل واحد بتثق فيه؟»

**Why it fits this case unusually well.** A جمعية is a machine made entirely of
trust with no paper in it. Asking four people who ran one for two years which of
them they trust least is the sharpest possible version of the question, and it
is a genuinely different question from *who moved the envelope*.

**Why that is also a problem.** See §17 risk 1 and §19. On the evidence, the
least trustworthy person in the room is **Souad** — she wrote **تم** next to a
lie forty-eight times. A table that understands the case perfectly may still,
correctly and thoughtfully, name her — and `interpretVote` will return
`MISSED_IMMEDIATE_TRUTH`. **This is the one unresolved design decision in the
specification.**

**Outcome handling** — unchanged from the engine, no new behaviour required:

| Vote outcome | `TruthResult` | Reveal |
|---|---|---|
| `DECIDED` → مصطفى | `FOUND_IMMEDIATE_TRUTH` | all 8 facts, same order |
| `DECIDED` → anyone else | `MISSED_IMMEDIATE_TRUTH` | all 8 facts, same order |
| `TIE` / `DEADLOCK` including مصطفى | `PARTIAL_TRUTH` | all 8 facts, same order |
| `TIE` / `DEADLOCK` excluding him | `MISSED_IMMEDIATE_TRUTH` | all 8 facts, same order |

One revote on a tie, then the room is allowed to have failed to agree — which
for this case is arguably the most honest ending available.

| Opening → E04 | مين كان عايز الـ٩٠٬٠٠٠؟ |
| E05 f1–f2 | محدش خدها. يبقى مين كان خايف من العدّ؟ |
| Facts 2–3 | مين كان خايف إن الـ٤٥٬٠٠٠ **تتفهم**؟ |
| Fact 7 | وهل كان نبيل غلطان إنه سأل؟ |

Fact 7 does **not** answer the last one. The table is meant to leave arguing
about it.

### 15.2 Why each layer lands

**Fact 1** is the reclassification and it is proved by geography, not testimony:
the money was in the flat. Nothing else needs to be said.

**Fact 3** inverts the object the table has been holding since minute three. The
same page, unchanged, reversed. This is the payoff for the numerical-fairness
work in §10 — the table did the arithmetic themselves, so the inversion happens
to *their* number.

**Fact 5** clears the person the table most likely accused, and clears her with
an object that was in their hands.

**Fact 6 is the recontextualization.** Hoda spends the whole evening as the most
morally certain person in the room — it is her money, she is owed, she is the
victim. Fact 6 does not make her a hypocrite. It makes her someone who has been
carried and never knew it, and it re-reads every angry line she said. It also
re-reads her *"خليهم يعدّوا"* at 8:13 as the single most costly sentence of the
night. **It costs the player nothing except a feeling, which is why it is last.**

**Fact 7 is not a moral.** It does not say Nabil was wrong to ask. It says what
asking cost. Case 001 closes on a chain of silence; Case 002 closes on the price
of transparency inside a system that only ever worked by not looking. **Same
product, opposite argument** — which is the clearest evidence that the two cases
are not the same case.

**Vote independence** — the same eight facts reveal in the same order whoever
was named. Only the one line describing what the room found changes.

## 16. Playability audit — four-player simulation

### 16.1 What each player knows before E01

| | Opening position |
|---|---|
| **سعاد** | Knows almost everything and can say almost none of it. Her opening move is to slow the room down. Reads as obstruction. |
| **هدى** | Knows the envelope was there at 7:40 and has already decided not to say so. Opens by accusing Nabil, which is loud and wrong. |
| **مصطفى** | Knows everything that matters and is the only person not theorising. **Visible.** His opening move is to be helpful and funny, which is exactly what he'd do anyway. |
| **نبيل** | Has a three-month-old theory, a real number, and a sighting he cannot cheaply produce. Opens by pressing Souad, which makes him the aggressor. |

Two players open by accusing (Hoda→Nabil, Nabil→Souad), one opens by delaying,
one opens by helping. **Nobody has to be prompted to talk.**

### 16.2 Theories reasonably formable before E01

From the premise alone — closed room, ٩٠٬٠٠٠, four people:
*Hoda needed it most* · *Souad had sole custody of the drawer* · *Nabil started
the whole thing and benefits from confusion* · *Mostafa was in and out*.
All four are alive at minute zero. None is supported yet. **Correct opening
state: maximum suspicion, minimum information.**

### 16.3 What changes after E02

E01 puts a number on the table; **E02 puts a grievance on it.** Before E02 the
room is arguing about custody; after E02 it is arguing about *fairness* — the
organiser quietly moved someone's turn and wrote "agreed verbally" in the
margin. Two shifts follow:

1. **Nabil stops being merely annoying and becomes sympathetic.** He was passed
   over. His demand for a count now reads as a wronged man asking a fair
   question, which makes T1 (Souad skimming) much more dangerous.
2. **The ١٢٬٠٠٠ becomes a date.** Any player who connects E02 f1 to E01 f3
   realises the covering started long before anything relevant to tonight — and
   that is the seed of fact 6, planted forty minutes before it flowers.

### 16.4 Can the group reconstruct independently? — the nine-step chain

1. Envelope present at 8:05 — **Souad, free to say.**
2. Found behind the water heater, unopened, ٧٥٬٠٠٠ — **E05 f1–f2.**
3. → Not a theft. Nobody wanted the money; somebody wanted the envelope out of
   the room.
4. The only thing that changed between 8:05 and 9:00 was the count demanded at
   8:10 — **all four know this, free.**
5. → The mover feared the count.
6. The count would have shown a shortfall of **exactly one share** — **E05 f2** —
   while the notebook says everyone paid — **E01 f2**. → The notebook is
   protecting somebody.
7. Someone has been supplying one share a month for three months — **E01 f3**,
   and those are the only three months paid in two parts — **E01 f2**.
8. Who? — **E04 f2** (external corroboration, three months) and **E04 f3**
   (6:52, before the evening began).
9. *Optional confirmation:* Nabil's 8:20 sighting.

**Nine steps. Every one supported by an object or a freely disclosable fact.
Step 9 is optional. A table that never persuades Nabil to admit where he was
standing still solves this case** — no player's willingness to be embarrassed is
a hard dependency, which is the fairness test that actually matters.

### 16.5 Why each suspect looks guilty

**نبيل** — He started it. He lies about his location within ten minutes. He was
passed over on the turn list and never said so. He wants out of the جمعية and
won't say why. He is the only person pushing hard, and people who push hard are
usually pushing away from something.

**سعاد** — Sole custody of the drawer. Refused a count **twice**, in front of
witnesses. Her handwriting is on both the perfect grid and the unexplained
money. She sold her gold three months ago and the building saw. She is not
searching very hard, and after a while that becomes visible.

**مصطفى** — In and out of the flat during the window. Lied about the charger.
E04 f1 reads, verbatim, like a handoff at 10pm. Broke, and hiding it badly. And
he is the only person in the room who never once offers a theory — the tell that
a sharp table will notice before any object confirms it.

**هدى** — Alone with the envelope. Enormous need. A receipt she can't explain.
And she lies, on the record, about being in the salon.

### 16.6 Believable alternative theory

**T5 — Souad hid it herself.** She was the only person who knew the envelope was
short; a count would expose her notebook; she controlled the drawer; she refused
the count twice; the money was found in her own flat. Every element is true. It
survives E05, it is defensible to the last minute, and it is wrong for exactly
one reason: **she never needed to hide it.** She could have delayed the handover
with a sentence.

**The engine's decision question makes T5 a *winning* answer to a different
question** — see §14 and risk 1.

### 16.7 Concealment vs. theft — can the group get there?

Yes, and by the shortest possible route. **E05 f1: the envelope is closed and
inside the flat.** That single observation converts theft to concealment without
any inference at all. It is not a deduction the table can miss; it is a physical
fact they are handed. The design question was never *can they get there* but
*when*, which is why E05 is gated four deep (§17 risk 4).

### 16.8 Does the reveal recontextualize?

| Object / moment | Read during play | Read after |
|---|---|---|
| E01 f2 — the perfect grid | Falsified records | Forty-eight acts of cover |
| E01 f3 — the untitled column | Money taken out | Money put in, from sold gold |
| E01 f2 — the double dates | Concealing a lag | Failing to raise it in time |
| E02 f2 — the turn swap | Favouritism | A booked hall and a fixed date |
| E03 f1 — the ٤٠٬٠٠٠ | She had money and lied | She borrowed at 10% |
| E04 f1 — "الساعة ١٠" | A handoff | A man being chased |
| E04 f3 — the unsent draft | Preparation | An abandoned confession |
| E05 f3 — the note | A forgery covering her | A promise never delivered |
| **Hoda's «خليهم يعدّوا»** | Reasonable | **The most expensive sentence of the night** |
| Souad's two refusals | Obstruction | Trying to protect two people at once |
| Behind the water heater | Where a thief hides money | Where a frightened man hides a question |

**Eleven inversions, every one from material the table held in its hands.**

## 17. Risks

**1 — The decision question does not match the immediate truth. (Highest.)**
`DECISION_QUESTION` is global: *"Who do you trust least?"* The immediate truth is
*who moved the envelope*. In Case 001 those two converge acceptably. In Case 002
they **actively diverge**: the most correct answer to "who moved it" is Mostafa,
and a strong argument for "who do you trust least" is Souad, who lied in writing
forty-eight times. A table that understands the case completely can be scored as
having missed it. **This is the unresolved design decision (§19).**

**2 — Mostafa must not read as stupid.** Mitigated hard in §11 (eight beats, the
6:52 timestamp, the geographic proof), but it remains the only psychological
link in an otherwise logical chain, and psychology is the one kind of link a
player is entitled to reject. **If the WS-3 writing does not land beat 5 — that
his fear is for Souad's exposure, not his own — the ending loses its warmth.**

**3 — The premise depends on knowing what a جمعية is.** One line in the intro
covers it for the target audience, and E01 is the first object on the table, so
the mechanism is shown before it is needed. Low risk domestically; a real
constraint on any future localisation.

**4 — E05 pacing.** The reclassification is the best beat in the case and it is
worthless early. The `requires` chain gates it behind four objects, but the
table controls tempo and a fast four could reach it in fifteen minutes.
*Content-side mitigation:* make E04 the heaviest object (three fragments, the
longest discussion prompt). *If that proves insufficient, an authored gate — "at
least one discussion beat must be spent between E04 and E05" — would need an
engine affordance.* **Noted, not requested.**

**5 — Nabil's unemployment is the thinnest thread.** It carries real weight
(explains his aggression, gives him a costly secret, humanises the accuser) but
it is not load-bearing for the solution, so underwriting it makes it read as a
convenience. Requires one early behavioural planting — the bag he carries down
every morning at 7:40 — or it lands as an excuse.

**6 — Ledger overlap with Case 001.** Both cases feature financial records that
do not reconcile. Raised in the WS-1 brief; the WS-2 architecture **widens the
separation** rather than narrowing it: Case 001's records were falsified for
self-protection years ago and an innocent woman publicly carried the blame; Case
002's are falsified as charity, are still being falsified tonight, and the
person they protect is in the room. Ending arguments are opposites (§15.2).
**Assessment: acceptable as designed. The WS-1 fallback variant is no longer
recommended** — removing the arithmetic would break §10, which is the spec's
strongest fairness guarantee.

**7 — Four characters, six members.** A visible seam. Both absences are ordinary
and both are stated in the intro (§5.2). If a table asks *why isn't everyone
here*, the answer must already have been given, not invented.

**8 — Tone drift toward misery.** Four adults humiliated about money for fifty
minutes. Counterweights are structural, not decorative: Mostafa is funny, Souad
is warm, Hoda is sharp, and the resolution is that everybody was being kind
badly. **No villain may emerge from the reveal, including Nabil.**

## 18. Egyptian cultural logic

Where the case is Egyptian **structurally** — the mechanics do not work without
these, and none is decoration.

| Mechanic | Social logic | Why the case fails without it |
|---|---|---|
| A جمعية with no contract | Trust between people who share a staircase is the security | There is no case if there is paperwork. The absence of a contract *is* the crime scene. |
| Souad covering silently | حفظ ماء الوجه — you spare someone the humiliation and never mention it | Her ٤٥٬٠٠٠ is inexplicable if she would simply have announced the shortfall |
| Writing **تم** anyway | Protecting a name is a real obligation, not a fraud impulse | Turns the notebook from evidence-of-fraud into evidence-of-decency, which is the case's central inversion |
| Mostafa's terror of the count | Being seen as broke on your own staircase is worse than being suspected | His whole action is irrational without it |
| His fear *for* Souad | العِشرة — his mother's closest friend; being the reason she's exposed is unbearable | Beat 5 of §11; without it he is merely self-serving |
| Nabil hiding unemployment | *عاطل* is an identity, not a status, and the building assigns it permanently | He would simply explain himself and the case collapses |
| Hoda refusing pity | Being the woman the building collects for is a demotion you never climb back from | Removes her reason to lie about 7:40 |
| The brother taking credit | A woman looked after by her family occupies a different rung than one who couldn't pay | Fact 6 has no weight |
| Nobody leaves the open room | Leaving is the accusation | The closed circle is social, not physical — this is what replaces the locked house |
| Souad refusing the count | *"الجمعية دي ماشية بالثقة"* is a real sentence with real force | The count would happen at 8:11 and there is no case |
| Selling gold quietly | Household gold is emergency capital; being seen at الصاغة is information | Gives the building a true observation and a wrong conclusion |
| Avoiding direct confrontation | Nobody says a name for the first twenty minutes | Produces the circling, hedging discussion the app exists to facilitate |

**Explicit anti-stereotype constraints for WS-3:** nobody is corrupt; nobody is
loud for local colour; no character explains Egyptian culture to another
character; no religious framing is used to signal authenticity; the neighbourhood
is neither picturesque nor squalid; and no one is poor — several people are one
bad month from *looking* poor, which is the actual subject.

## 20. Recommendation

# **PASS**

The architecture holds. The ٤٥٬٠٠٠ is derivable from objects in eight steps
without Nabil (§10); the case reconstructs in nine steps of which one is optional
(§16.4); no fact rests on a single object or a single player's willingness to be
embarrassed; and every misdirection is a true statement with a missing header.

**One decision must be made before WS-3 writing begins — see §21.**

**Strongest gameplay mechanic** — **the reclassification at E05.** The moment the
envelope is found closed, inside the flat, short by exactly one share, every
player's model inverts at once and the question changes from *who wanted ٩٠٬٠٠٠*
to *who was afraid of ٤٥٬٠٠٠ being understood*. It is not a deduction the table
can miss — it is a physical fact handed to them — and it converts an ordinary
whodunit into the only interesting question in the case, with twenty minutes
still on the clock.

**Weakest gameplay mechanic** — **the 7:40 disclosure loop.** Hoda's sighting is
the cheapest of the four decisive facts to reason around (the 8:05 sighting
supersedes it entirely), so a table can simply route past it, and a player
holding a costly fact that nobody needs is a player with nothing to trade. It is
the one place where the disclosure economy has slack. *Mitigation available in
WS-3 writing rather than architecture:* have Hoda's 7:40 fragment carry a second
detail — that the elastic band was already on the envelope — which is the only
independent corroboration that Souad's note predates the disappearance, and would
make her sighting genuinely load-bearing for clearing Souad. **Recommended, not
yet applied.**

## 21. Unresolved design decision

**The decision question versus the immediate truth.**

`DECISION_QUESTION = 'Who do you trust least?'` is a single global constant
([`src/engine/voting.ts:30`](src/engine/voting.ts:30)), and `interpretVote` grades
the room against `immediateAnswerCharacterId`. For Case 002 those two things
point at different people: *who moved the envelope* is Mostafa; *who do you trust
least*, on the evidence, has a serious case for Souad.

This is not a flaw in the case — it is the case working correctly and the
engine's one global sentence not being able to express it. Three options:

| Option | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A — accept it** | none | Some tables that fully understand the case are told they missed it. Cheapest, and the least honest. |
| **B — per-case decision question** | one optional field on `CaseDefinition`, one fallback to the constant; no engine logic changes | Case 002 asks **«مين اللي شال الظرف؟»**; Case 001 is unaffected. **Recommended.** |
| **C — author around it** | free, and constraining | Write Souad's briefing to make her *feel* trustworthy so the room doesn't name her — i.e. suppress a legitimate reading to fit a sentence. Rejected. |

**Recommendation: B.** It is a content-layer field, it does not touch the phase
machine, the reducer, voting, or truth interpretation, and it leaves Case 001
byte-identical. **Flagged for approval — not implemented, and no code has been
changed.**

## 23. Inspiration notes

Structural technique only. No characters, names, dialogue, scenes, plot
sequences, settings, or twists are drawn from any existing work.

- **Closed circle by social constraint** — the exit is open; using it is the
  confession.
- **Asymmetric testimony** — no witness holds a complete timeline; the timeline
  is an assembled object.
- **Costly disclosure** — the most valuable fact is held by the person most
  humiliated by explaining how they know it.
- **Financial misdirection by omitted direction** — a correct figure, an
  inverted arrow. The page has no header; nothing on it is false.
- **Reclassification of the crime mid-investigation** — recovery of the property
  changes the question rather than answering it.
- **Staged object reading** — an object whose first fragment builds a complete
  false theory and whose later fragments dismantle it.
- **The discredited true witness** — a witness lies about a trivial detail for a
  private reason, then must be believed about a decisive one.
- **Delayed benefactor reveal** — a kindness attributed to the wrong person for
  years.
- **Final recontextualization of a moral position** — the most righteous voice
  in the room was standing on an unknown debt.
- **Motive detached from the prize** — the answer has the weakest apparent
  motive because the real motive is exposure, not money.
